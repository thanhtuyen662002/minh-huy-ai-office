The identity is stable across replacement claim leases for the same original
input. A quarantined source dependency or changed selected brain business version
changes the identity even when the provider request remains identical. New input
is distinct; a replacement worker alone cannot reset the same input's budget.

The internal reducer consumes at most three ordered starts and three failures.
It requires exact scope, batch and identity, dense ordinals, unique request IDs,
the original predecessor request, positive claim epoch, canonical UTC and an
attempt deadline greater than its start and at most two minutes later. A start
without a known response consumes an attempt until its original deadline.

permanent provider failure forbids successors. After three starts, the last

active third attempt produces `Wait`. Earlier starts cannot be omitted to reset
the count, and a successor cannot precede the previous observation/deadline plus
backoff. Malformed journals refuse with a fixed inner-free message.
